Primary Target User: 
Everyday readers, patients, and non-expert researchers who need help understanding dense, educational health information written in highly techincal language. 

Target User: 
Persona Name: Alex 

Demographics: Adults managing personal health conditions, family caregivers, or general readers researching wellness topics online.

Background: Comfortable using basic web applications, but lacks a background in clinical science, biology, or medicine.


What are they trying to do?

Read and comprehend online health articles, educational medical guides, or patient reference materials.

Quickly extract key takeaways regarding symptoms, wellness advice, or health concepts without getting bogged down by medical terminology.

Verify that the information they are reading is accurate and grounded in reliable original sources. 

What frustrates them?

Dense Medical Jargon: Feeling overwhelmed by technical vocabulary (e.g., idiopathic, asymptomatic, etiology) that requires constant dictionary lookups.

Complex Sentence Structure: Reading long, passive-voice clinical explanations that obscure simple facts.

Unreliable Summaries: Chatbots or web searches that output oversimplified answers without citing or linking back to the original source text.

What do they need? 
They need clear, direct explanations of health concepts written at an accessible reading level.

Instant definitions for unfamiliar vocabulary within the context of the passage.

Clear line-by-line or reference links showing exactly where the simplified information came from in the original source text.

What they shouldn't expect from our application:
Medical Diagnosis or Clinical Advice
Replacement for Healthcare Providers
Generative Content Without Input
Emergency Health Assistance
