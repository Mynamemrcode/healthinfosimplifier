# Safety and Scope Contract - HealthInfo Simplifier

## 1. Purpose and Core Objective
The HealthInfo Simplifier is an educational tool designed to help users understand general health information by breaking down complex medical terminology and summarizing dense articles. It is built strictly for learning and educational exploration.

## 2. Safety Boundaries (What the Application WILL NOT Do)
To ensure safety and ethical AI usage, this application explicitly enforces the following restrictions:
- Will NOT diagnose diseases or medical conditions.
- Will NOT interpret user symptoms as a medical diagnosis.
- Will NOT prescribe medication or treatments.
- Will NOT tell a user to start, stop, or change a medication dosage.
- Will NOT create personalized treatment plans.
- Will NOT handle medical emergencies or act as a triage service.
- Will NOT store private medical records or personal health information.
- Will NOT replace professional medical advice, consultation, or care.

## 3. Data Privacy and Boundaries
- Permitted Data: Public educational articles, synthetic health examples, and non-sensitive test passages.
- Strictly Prohibited Data: Real patient records, medical histories, personal health documents, or clinical datasets. No user-entered private health data will ever be stored or logged.

## 4. AI Limitations and Disclaimer
AI models can hallucinate or misinterpret nuanced context. All simplified outputs must preserve factual accuracy, include explicit source attribution, and display a persistent medical disclaimer reminding users to consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
